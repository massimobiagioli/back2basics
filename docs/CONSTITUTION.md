# Back 2 Basics — Project Constitution

## Mission

Back 2 Basics is a **Micro-Learning platform** designed to create and deliver **focused playbooks**
for mastering IT topics. Each playbook distills a subject down to its essence: no filler, no fluff,
just the concepts that matter, explained with clarity and precision.

## Core Principles

### 1. Straight to the Point

Every piece of content must answer the question *"What do I actually need to know?"*
Avoid verbose introductions, historical digressions, and tangential anecdotes.
If a sentence does not carry actionable insight, it does not belong.

### 2. Build on Existing Foundations

The audience is **not a beginner**. They already have a grounding in computer science and
software engineering. Content must respect this: skip the absolute basics of computing
(variables, loops, what a CPU is) and focus on reinforcing, deepening, and connecting
concepts the learner already vaguely knows but has not truly mastered.

### 3. Playbook Format

Content is organized into **playbooks** — self-contained, sequential learning paths.
A playbook:
- Has a clear, specific goal (e.g. "Understand BGP routing").
- Is broken into short, digestible sections.
- Favors diagrams, comparisons, and worked examples over walls of text.
- Includes a "Check Your Understanding" checkpoint at key milestones.

### 4. Simplicity Over Completeness

Prefer a crisp explanation that captures 80% of the truth over an exhaustive treatise
that captures 100%. Nuance and edge cases belong in footnotes or "Deep Dive" sidebars
that the reader can choose to explore — never in the critical path.

### 5. Progressive Depth

Structure content in layers:
1. **The one-liner** — a single sentence that captures the idea.
2. **The core explanation** — the minimum viable understanding.
3. **The connections** — how this idea relates to other ideas in the playbook and beyond.
4. **The deep dive** — optional, for those who want more.

### 6. Consistent Voice

All content follows a unified tone:
- **Direct** — use active voice, short sentences.
- **Conversational** — write as if explaining to a colleague over coffee.
- **Precise** — use correct technical terminology; never dumb down.
- **Humble** — acknowledge when a topic has nuance or competing interpretations.

## Scope

Back 2 Basics covers the broad landscape of **Information Technology**, including but not
limited to:

- Software Architecture & Design Patterns
- Networking & Protocols
- Operating Systems & Concurrency
- Databases & Storage
- Distributed Systems
- Cloud & Infrastructure
- Security Fundamentals
- Programming Paradigms

## Governance

- The constitution is the **highest-authority document** of the project.
- Every playbook, page, and contribution must align with these principles.
- Amendments require a clear rationale and must preserve the spirit of the original mission.
- When in doubt, re-read Principle #1.
